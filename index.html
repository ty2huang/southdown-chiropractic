Meet the heart of Southdown

Dr. Paul Zmiyiwsky, “Dr. Z”

For over 26 years, Dr. Paul Zmiyiwsky, known to many as Dr. Z, has been helping patients live healthier, pain-free lives.

As the founder of Southdown Chiropractic & Wellness, Dr. Z has built a space where compassion, experience and results come together. He takes the time to understand each patient's unique concerns and creates personalized treatment plans that support their long-term health.

Whether you're recovering from an injury, managing ongoing discomfort or looking to stay well, he's here to help every step of the way.

  • Personalized

    A plan built around your body, your history and your goals.

  • Evidence-based

    Proven, hands-on care with no reliance on drugs or surgery.

  • Holistic

    Chiropractic, massage and mind-body care working together.

New patients

What to expect at your first visit

Your first appointment is all about getting to know you and understanding your health. Every step is designed to help us understand your needs and give you personalized care.

  1. 01

    Health history

    Complete a short set of health history forms so we understand your concerns, lifestyle and goals.

  2. 02

    Meet Dr. Paul Zmiyiwsky

    Sit down one-on-one to talk through what you're feeling, followed by a thorough assessment.

  3. 03

    Digital spinal X-rays

    When appropriate, we take digital X-rays on-site, so there's no separate trip to an imaging clinic.

  4. 04

    Your first adjustment

    You'll start feeling the difference with your first treatment, on the very first day.

  5. 05

    Your personalized plan

    At your follow-up, we review your X-rays in detail, explain our findings and map out a treatment plan tailored to you.
